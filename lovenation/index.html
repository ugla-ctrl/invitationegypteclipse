Why Love Nation, why now

Two gatherings built around music and story.

Stages made for your mission

Two to three headline acts each night at Coherence, music after totality on the Nile, and stories told across both. Love Nation’s artists and storytellers would have a home on every one of those stages.

Two great rivers, one story

Coherence gathers on the Colorado River in Austin and closes on World Water Day. Egypt follows the Nile to the longest totality on land for the next 87 years. Water is the thread between them, and a story worth telling.

Audiences ready to be united

Iceland Eclipse 2026 brought together 4,200 people from 75 countries beneath totality, and over 1,100 of them bought VIP. Coherence and Egypt carry that community forward: people who come to take part, not just to watch.

Contribution you can see

Part of every dollar holds seats for artists, storytellers and changemakers who couldn’t otherwise be there, named in Love Nation’s honor. Through our impact partner, we will also remove 10,000 pounds of ocean-bound plastic.

Our community

An audience that comes to listen, share and take part.

Our gatherings draw founders, artists, healers and creatives in their thirties and forties who travel for experiences that change them, then go home and tell the story. That is the audience Love Nation exists to bring together.

Three days of music, meditation ceremonies, panels, wellness lounges, and collective action on the Colorado River in Austin. Saturday on the Spring Equinox looks at the most optimistic future for humanity over the next 10 to 15 years. Sunday under the Full Moon is about optimizing mind, body, heart and spirit. Monday, World Water Day, explores the technology and consciousness of water, a thread that runs straight to the Nile in August. Two to three headline acts play each night.

Included · $50,000 valuePresenting Sponsorship

  • “Love Nation presents Coherence”, category exclusive, named at the launch announcement and in every moment through the recap film
  • Media: title partner on the press release and press kit, logo on all creative, a dedicated social campaign, and a full page in the festival guide and app
  • On site: major logo at the gate and the main stage, named on every wristband, app push notifications, and the shuttle program
  • Hospitality: 8 VIP and 20 GA passes, 2 RV Village pads, seats at the artist and speaker dinner, and 4 all-areas production passes
  • Activation: a Love Nation story lounge on site, a song and story set before the headliners each night, and the keynote introduction on one themed day

Every nightly story set is filmed and delivered to Love Nation, so the stories told in Austin keep reaching new audiences long after the weekend.

The investment$250,000 of the $500,000 round

  • Preferred membership interest. Investors receive 80% of profits until their capital is returned plus a competitive hurdle return, then keep 20% of profits.
  • Love Nation’s share is pro rata: 40% of profits until $250,000 plus the hurdle is returned, then 10% ongoing.
  • Use of funds: the round covers most of the $528K of deposits and pre-event costs due before ticket sales carry the load.

A seven-night Nile journey from Aswan to Luxor and back, the Eclipse Gathering on Luxor’s West Bank, and three days at the Pyramids closing with the 8/8 Lion’s Gate at the Sphinx. Artists, scientists, astronauts and founders travel together, with temple visits at dawn and music after totality.

Included · $100,000 valueAnchor Partnership

  • Named Anchor Partner of Egypt Eclipse across every chapter of the journey, in the press release and press kit
  • A signature experience carrying Love Nation’s name, chosen together: a live music and storytelling evening after totality, Dawn Balloons over the Valley of the Kings, or the Lion’s Gate at Giza
  • Content: credits in the official film and recap, features across the Eclipse community’s social and newsletters, and full photo and video coverage delivered after
  • Presence: a bespoke activation at the Eclipse Gathering, welcome-kit seeding, and a private hosted evening for Love Nation guests

The Celebrity is our most curated boat: a hand-picked circle of artists, scientists and founders on the seven-night journey from Aswan to Luxor and back. Every place includes the Nile journey, temple visits with Egyptologists, the programming on board and the Eclipse Gathering in Luxor.

  • Presence: patron-only dinners, sunrise walks and time with the founders and featured co-creators, plus the three-day Giza Activation for the suite
  • Voice: a seat at the design table for Egypt and the gatherings that follow, and a music and story session on board that carries Love Nation’s name
  • Legacy: recognition as an Anchor Patron of the Eclipse Trilogy, and sponsored seats held in Love Nation’s name for artists, storytellers and changemakers
  • For your community: priority access for Love Nation’s artists and supporters to join the journey, by arrangement

Patronage underwrites the artists we commission, the scientists we host, the production that shapes the experience, and seats for people who couldn’t otherwise be in the room.
