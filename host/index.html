For Hosts · Role and scope

Trusted to hold the journey.

What it means to host your community in Egypt, on any pathway.

Hosts are hand-selected because they belong to, or best represent, the community a pathway is built for. Every pathway has them: the Nile cruise, the Marsam, the Omda, the Gathering and the Path of Totality aboard the Jaz Celebrity. A Host is part guide, part steward, part crew, and the person their community trusts to make the journey feel like theirs.

01How Hosts show up

Hosts don't carry set responsibilities. Every function below has its own support on the trip. Hosts are trusted members of the community, so when gaps inevitably come up along the journey, they're the people who can step up and fill them.

  1. Holding space. Setting the tone for their group, welcoming people in, and offering a steady presence when a moment needs one.

  2. Master of ceremonies. Opening or closing a session, leading introductions and transitions, or carrying an announcement when it helps the day flow.

  3. Production. Lending a hand where the Egyptian teams (ENEV, the ship and hotel staff) and our production crew leave a gap. People in our community are often best placed to help with sound, staging, setup and timing.

  4. Guest care. Helping guests feel looked after and informed, and passing anything they need to their Egypt Eclipse point of contact early.

02Staying connected

Hosts are welcome on both the community calls and the core team calls. Joining them is how Hosts stay close to the plan, share what their community needs, and work alongside the other Hosts and the production team long before we arrive in Egypt.

03Bringing your people

Each Host is expected to bring their community with them, to the Gathering and to the trip as a whole. The number of guests is agreed with each Host up front. Every guest they invite receives a discount on their place, and Hosts are thanked for each place booked, as set out in their agreement.

  • The minimum number of guests depends on the pathway and is set in each Host's agreement.

04First rights to create

Because Hosts commit early and give this much to the journey, they are among the first offered the chance to contribute content and creative work to it. This is the same early access we give to those who were first to make their financial commitment to a pathway.

05Working with the team, and what Hosts receive

Hosts work within the program and alongside the production lead, who keeps final say on logistics and safety. They follow site rules and the guidance of ENEV and the ship and hotel teams, and raise concerns with their Egypt Eclipse point of contact first.

What Hosts receive

In return, Hosts receive the standard Host rate of 50% off their pathway, with about five passes sold on that pathway covering the rest so the experience is fully comped, plus recognition on the pathway page, and the first rights to create described above. The full terms sit in each Host's agreement.

The Path of Totality aboard the Jaz Celebrity has its own Host terms.
