Collaboration proposal

Back to Egypt

In December 2012 you took The Great Convergence to the pyramids and down the Nile. We'd like to invite you back for the next great celestial gathering for the total solar eclipse over Luxor on August 2, 2027.

Totality lasts 6 minutes 23 seconds, the longest on land this century. Around 500 people travel with us, by Nile cruise or on Luxor's West Bank, then on to Giza. We'd love the Do LaB to be part of it.

Where we see you

I · Build

Eclipse day is outdoors in August heat. We need shade and a stage on the Gathering field next to the Marsam hotel.

II · Curate

Take a night: the celebration after totality on August 2, or the Lion's Gate at the Giza pyramids on August 8.

III · Host

Bring your crew as a group on one of the Nile boats or at a Luxor hotel, and host that part of the journey.

IV · Share

Tell the Lightning in a Bottle community about Egypt, and we'll tell ours about LIB. The dates don't overlap.
