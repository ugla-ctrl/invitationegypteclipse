Collaboration proposal

Back to Egypt

In December 2012 you took The Great Convergence to the pyramids and down the Nile. Fifteen years on, we'd love to bring you back for what we think will be one of the most talked-about gatherings of the decade.

Around the total solar eclipse over Luxor on August 2, 2027, we're producing world-class experiential moments at some of the world's most sacred sites: a New Moon Ceremony, 6 minutes 23 seconds of totality, a celebration that night, a concert at the Temple of Hatshepsut (planned), and the Lion's Gate at the pyramids of Giza. Around 500 people travel with us, by Nile cruise or on Luxor's West Bank, and the circle is deliberately high-touch: artists, builders, thinkers and community leaders who come as co-creators.

It's still early. The program is being shaped now, and we can feature the Do LaB to whatever degree feels right, from a single night to the setting for the whole Gathering. We want to create something intimate, festival-inspired and unforgettable with you.

From the team behind Iceland Eclipse 2026, which brought people from more than 75 countries together for totality.
